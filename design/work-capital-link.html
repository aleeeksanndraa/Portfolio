01the problem

Renting their own flats through someone else's platform.

Capital Link manages high-end serviced apartments across Canary Wharf, Mayfair and the City - but every booking ran through Airbnb and Booking.com. That meant commission on each stay, no control over the brand, and no direct line to their guests.

For guests, the third-party experience was worse: confusing pricing, hidden fees, inconsistent information that undercut trust at exactly the moment someone is about to spend £400+ a night. The brief - a platform of their own: premium, transparent, synced to their Hostaway listings.

02role & scope

Led the full product, end to end.

  • Led the full multi-page product - research and strategy through final UI delivery.
  • Set the brand identity - logo direction, monochrome palette and type system.
  • Designed for Hostaway sync and handed off a dev-ready system to the web team.
03research

Three insights shaped everything.

Working with the client, I defined guest personas - business travellers, digital nomads and families - mapped booking behaviour, and audited Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and Plum Guide to find the gaps a premium operator could own.

01

Clarity & trust

Guests value transparency above all. Hidden fees break confidence - clear pricing builds it back.

02

Hotel-grade confidence

High-income users expect fast support, professional presentation and privacy. Booking a flat should feel as safe as a hotel.

03

Flexibility

Short stay to several months, solo to family - the platform has to flex without feeling complicated.

04design principles

Three rules for the experience.

principle 01

Reassurance at every step

The journey mirrors a hotel - location maps, photo-led browsing, clear policies - so confidence builds as the guest moves forward.

principle 02

Price you can trust

Nightly rate, cleaning fee and total shown up front. No surprises at checkout - transparency is the product.

principle 03

Quietly premium

A monochrome system and editorial photography let the apartments speak. Elegant, never loud.

05the flow

Explore → select → book.

I shaped the platform around a clean homepage funnel - explore, select, book - with smart filters for dates, size and amenities, real-time Hostaway availability, and a focused mobile checkout. Each stage asks for one decision at a time, so a high-value booking never feels heavy.

06key screens

The screens I refined most.

screen 01

Listings & filters

Card-style property tiles with hover feedback, smart filters and sort - fast to scan, easy to narrow.

screen 02

Property details

Photo gallery, amenities, reviews and an interactive map - everything that answers "can I trust this?"

screen 03

Booking summary

Date picker with live availability and a transparent price breakdown - rate, cleaning fee, total.

screen 04

Confirm & pay

A simple, mobile-optimised checkout with clear payment methods and a sign-in-to-book step.

07branding & identity

Elegant, trustworthy, modern.

The identity reads elegant, trustworthy and modern. An architectural line-mark, generous white space and a muted monochrome palette position Capital Link firmly at the premium end.

  • Architectural skyline logomark for a property brand.
  • Monochrome base - eerie black through platinum.
  • Lato for clean, confident type throughout.
  • Full-bleed, editorial apartment photography.
08design system

A system built to scale.

A tokenised, state-complete UI kit - buttons, inputs, cards, calendars and booking modules - consistent across desktop and mobile and ready for the dev team to build on.

  • Card, filter, calendar and booking-summary components.
  • Full interaction states - default, hover, active, disabled.
  • Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet and desktop.

10reflection

Trust is built in the details.

For a luxury booking, confidence isn't won by a hero image - it's won by the small, honest moments: a price with nothing hidden behind it, a real map, a review you believe. Restraint did more than decoration ever could. Designing for a transparent price breakdown early settled dozens of layout decisions downstream.
