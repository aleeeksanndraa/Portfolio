03

applied graphics

istituto marangoni · 2026

From difference comes design.

A design proposal for the Istituto Marangoni tote bag, academic year 2026–27 - a globally distributed object that carries the institution's identity across its international campuses.

The concept translates Marangoni's global presence into one minimal visual system: a single continuous line connecting the creative capitals where its campuses live.

Milano—Firenze—Paris—London—Dubai—Riyadh—Shanghai—Shenzhen—Mumbai—Miami

One → continuous → line

The idea began as a single hand sketch - one stroke shaping multiple landmarks while holding balance and clarity. It reflects connection, continuity and the flow of ideas across cultures, echoing Istituto Marangoni's expanding international network.

04

ai influencer experiment

erro_0rr · Angel404Shop · AI character worldbuilding

An AI-made singer, influencer and her own jewelry world.

erro_0rr was an AI-made singer and influencer created as an experiment in digital identity, AI-generated content and audience reaction. The project explored how people respond when an artificial character is presented as a full online persona - with music, videos, social media presence and a connected jewelry shop.

Angel404Shop extended erro_0rr's world into a product direction, selling jewelry in her style through a darker, more cinematic identity. The project included character visuals, song promotion, jewelry imagery, Instagram feed direction and a website for the shop.
