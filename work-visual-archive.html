visual archive

a curated archive

Posters, logos, printed objects and visual identity experiments.

intro

Typography, composition, mood and identity.

A curated archive of visual work across posters, logo explorations, printed objects and brand experiments.

This section collects smaller visual projects that focus on typography, composition, mood, identity and social-first design.

categories

four sections
01 — posters
Event visuals, campaign posters and social-first compositions.
02 — logofolio
Logo marks, wordmarks and identity explorations.
03 — tote bag / merch
Printed object design and applied visual identity.
04 — experiments
Additional visual studies, typography tests and brand directions.
01

posters

A selection of poster and campaign visuals exploring typography, rhythm, contrast and mood. These works focus on creating immediate attention while keeping the composition clear and visually memorable.

02

logofolio

A collection of logo marks and wordmark explorations created across different brand directions. The focus is on simple, flexible identity elements that can work across digital and printed applications.

03

tote bag / merchandise

Printed object concepts exploring how visual identity can move beyond the screen. This section includes tote bag and merchandise-style applications where typography, logo placement and composition become part of the object.

04

experiments

A small selection of visual studies, layout tests and identity directions. These pieces show my interest in atmosphere, cultural references, typography and expressive digital visuals.

closing note

The more experimental side of my practice.

The visual archive shows a more experimental side of my practice — where I explore mood, typography, identity and composition outside of full product case studies.
