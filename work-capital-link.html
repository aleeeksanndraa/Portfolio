capital link

luxury serviced-apartment booking platform

A direct-booking platform for luxury serviced apartments in London - built to free Capital Link from Airbnb and Booking.com, and to make booking a flat online feel as trustworthy as checking into a hotel.

01

the problem

Renting their own flats through someone else's platform.

Capital Link manages high-end serviced apartments across Canary Wharf, Mayfair and the City - but every booking ran through Airbnb and Booking.com. That meant commission on each stay, no control over the brand, and no direct line to their own guests.

For guests, the third-party experience was worse: confusing pricing, hidden fees, and inconsistent information that undercut trust at exactly the moment someone is about to spend £400+ a night. The brief was a platform of their own - premium, transparent, and synced to their Hostaway listings.

03

what luxury guests expect

Three insights shaped everything.

Working with the client, I defined the guest personas - business travellers, digital nomads and families - mapped booking behaviour, and audited Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and Plum Guide to find the gaps a premium operator could own.

01

Clarity & trust

Guests value transparency above all. Hidden fees and inconsistent listings break confidence - clear pricing builds it back.

02

Hotel-grade confidence

High-income users expect fast support, professional presentation and privacy. Booking a flat should feel as safe as a hotel.

03

Flexibility

Short stay to several months, solo to family - the platform has to flex without ever feeling complicated.

04

three principles for the design

principle 01

Reassurance at every step

The journey mirrors a hotel - location maps, photo-led browsing, clear policies - so confidence builds as the guest moves forward.

principle 02

Price you can trust

Nightly rate, cleaning fee and total shown up front. No surprises at checkout - transparency is the product.

principle 03

Quietly premium

A monochrome system and editorial photography let the apartments speak. Elegant, never loud.

05

structuring the booking journey

Explore → Select → Book

I shaped the platform around a clean homepage funnel - explore, select, book - with smart filters for dates, size and amenities, real-time Hostaway availability, and a focused mobile checkout. Each stage asks for one decision at a time, so a high-value booking never feels heavy.

06

the screens i refined most

screen 01

Listings & filters

Card-style property tiles with hover feedback, smart filters and sort - fast to scan, easy to narrow.

screen 02

Property details

Photo gallery, amenities, reviews and an interactive map - everything that answers "can I trust this?"

screen 03

Booking summary

Date picker with live availability and a transparent price breakdown - rate, cleaning fee, total.

screen 04

Confirm & pay

A simple, mobile-optimised checkout with clear payment methods and a sign-in-to-book step.

07

branding & identity

The identity reads elegant, trustworthy and modern. An architectural line-mark, generous white space and a muted monochrome palette position Capital Link firmly at the premium end.

  • Architectural skyline logomark for a property brand.
  • Monochrome base - eerie black through platinum.
  • Lato for clean, confident type throughout.
  • Full-bleed, editorial apartment photography.
08

a system built to scale

A tokenised, state-complete UI kit - buttons, inputs, cards, calendars and booking modules - consistent across desktop and mobile and ready for the dev team to build on.

  • Card, filter, calendar and booking-summary components.
  • Full interaction states - default, hover, active, disabled.
  • Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet and desktop.

10

what i learned

Trust is built in the details.

For a luxury booking, confidence isn't won by a hero image - it's won by the small, honest moments: a price with nothing hidden behind it, a real map, a review you believe. Restraint did more than decoration ever could. Designing for a transparent price breakdown early settled dozens of layout decisions downstream, and kept the whole product feeling as premium as the apartments it sells.
