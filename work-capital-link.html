capital link

fintech website

A marketing site and visual identity for a capital-markets platform — responsive, trust-forward, and built from the ground up in Webflow.

02

overview

Serious money deserves a serious interface.

Capital Link needed a website that signalled credibility to institutional clients while staying approachable enough to convert. I handled the identity direction and the full site design and build.

The result balances restraint and confidence — generous space, precise type, and motion used only where it earns attention.

04

challenge

Credible, not cold.

Finance sites often read as either intimidating or generic. Capital Link wanted to feel established and trustworthy without the corporate stiffness.

I leaned on editorial typography and disciplined spacing to project authority, then warmed it with considered motion and a confident accent.

06

The site guides an institutional visitor from credibility to contact in a single, unhurried scroll — proof, product, then a clear path to talk.

  • Narrative homepage that builds trust before it asks for anything.
  • Reusable section blocks editable through the Webflow CMS.
  • One unmistakable primary action repeated at natural decision points.
