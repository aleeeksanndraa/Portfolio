philosophy

(how i work)
(01)

typography first

Most design problems are typography problems wearing a costume. Get the type system, hierarchy, and rhythm right and the interface starts explaining itself.

(02)

systems over screens

A beautiful screen is an anecdote; a design system is an argument. Variables, tokens, and component libraries mean the hundredth screen ships as sharp as the first.

(03)

craft survives handoff

Design isn't done in Figma — it's done when it ships. Dev Mode, accessibility standards, and tight engineering collaboration are part of the work, not an afterthought.
